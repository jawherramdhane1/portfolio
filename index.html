CASE STUDY 04

Théâtre — Avenue du Spectacle

Live entertainment · Event promotion · Paid acquisition strategy

META ADS CAMPAIGN

The objective

Promote cultural and live entertainment experiences by reaching relevant audiences and moving high-intent users toward registration or participation.

My performance approach

  • Define the conversion objective before campaign execution.
  • Build audience segments around the event, artist and cultural interests.
  • Test multiple creative angles instead of relying on one visual.
  • Analyze CTR, CPC, landing-page engagement, cost per result and frequency.
  • Reallocate budget toward the strongest audience + creative combinations.

Cold audience → Engagement → High intent → Registration / Conversion
