SecomPlus — Customer Acquisition Case Study

Meta Ads · TikTok Ads

Objective

Scale monthly revenue through paid acquisition while keeping a profitable ROAS as ad spend increased.

Target Audience

French and Western European online shoppers, interest- and lookalike-based targeting built around the store's core product category.

Challenge

Rising CPMs and creative fatigue on existing ad sets were pushing CPA up and slowing new-customer acquisition.

Strategy & Campaign Structure

Audience segmentation, prospecting vs. retargeting split, and structured budget allocation across cold and warm audiences.

Creative Testing

Iterative A/B testing across ad formats, hooks, and angles to identify top-performing creatives.

Budget Optimization

Continuous reallocation toward top-performing ad sets based on CPA and ROAS thresholds.
